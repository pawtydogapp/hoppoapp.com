How do I look after my baby's teeth?

Start brushing as soon as the first tooth appears. Good habits early protect the milk teeth.

What is common

  • Brush at least twice a day, once just before bed.
  • Use fluoride toothpaste: a smear the size of a grain of rice for babies and toddlers, and a pea-sized amount from about 2 to 3 years. Ask your dentist which amount is advised where you live.
  • Children need an adult to help or check their brushing until at least age 7.

What can help

  • Brush in small circles over every tooth.
  • Let your child spit out the toothpaste, but do not rinse their mouth with water.
  • Bottles are for milk or water only, never juice or sweet drinks.
  • From 6 months, offer drinks in an open or free-flowing cup.
  • Keep sweet foods and drinks to meal times.
  • Take your child along to your own dental check-ups so they get used to the dentist.

Talk to your doctor, midwife or health visitor if

  • You see white or brown spots on the teeth.
  • Your child has tooth pain or swollen gums.
  • You want to find a dentist for your child.

Get urgent help now if

  • A knocked-out or broken tooth after a fall. Contact a dentist the same day.

Call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department.

This guide is general information, not medical advice. Trust your instinct. If you are worried, contact your doctor, midwife or health visitor. This guide is checked against the NHS, the World Health Organization (WHO), the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) and other public health sources.
