How much screen time is OK?

The World Health Organization (WHO) recommends sitting less and playing more for children under 5.

What is common

  • Under 2 years: screen time is not recommended, except video calls with family.
  • At 2 years: no more than 1 hour a day. Less is better.
  • From 3 to 4 years: no more than 1 hour a day.
  • From 1 to 4 years, children need at least 3 hours of active play spread over the day.

What can help

  • Watch together and talk about what you see.
  • Keep screens out of meals and the hour before bed.
  • Swap some screen time for books, songs and play.
  • Avoid keeping children strapped in a pram or seat for more than 1 hour at a time.

Talk to your doctor, midwife or health visitor if

  • Screens are causing a lot of conflict at home.
  • You are worried about your child's sleep, speech or play.

This guide is general information, not medical advice. Trust your instinct. If you are worried, contact your doctor, midwife or health visitor. This guide is checked against the NHS, the World Health Organization (WHO), the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) and other public health sources.
