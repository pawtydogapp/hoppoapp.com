Questions

Is Hoppo really free?

Yes. Every skill, guide and feature is open to everyone. A small banner advert pays for the servers. Some links in the Marketplace are affiliate links, which means Hoppo may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Is Hoppo medical advice?

No. Hoppo gives practice ideas, keeps your memories and explains what is common. It does not replace your doctor, midwife or health visitor. If you are worried about your child, contact them. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

My baby was born early. Which age does Hoppo use?

Open your child's profile in More and tick "Born early", then enter the original due date. Missions then follow the corrected age until age 2.

My baby has arrived. How do I switch from pregnancy?

Hoppo shows "Has your baby arrived?" on Home in the last 4 weeks of the pregnancy. Before that, open More, then Children, then your pregnancy profile, and tap "Has your baby arrived?". Enter the date of birth. Hoppo turns the pregnancy into your baby's profile and moves to the first year.
