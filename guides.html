"Is this normal?" guides

35 short guides for the questions parents ask most. Each one says what is common, what can help, and when to talk to your doctor, midwife or health visitor.

The same guides are in the Hoppo app, word for word. They are general information, not medical advice. Each is checked against the NHS, the World Health Organization (WHO), the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) and other public health sources.

Pregnancy

I feel sick all the time

Feeling sick or being sick is very common in early pregnancy.

I am so tired and cannot sleep

Tiredness is very common, especially in the first 12 weeks and again near the end.

My baby is moving less

Getting to know your baby's movements is one of the most important things you can do in pregnancy.

What do I pack for the birth?

Pack your bag at least 3 weeks before your due date.

Is this labour?

Labour can start slowly.

What should I not eat in pregnancy?

Most foods are fine in pregnancy.

Can I exercise while pregnant?

Staying active is good for you and your baby.

Baby, 0 to 12 months

My baby cries a lot

All babies cry, and some cry much more than others.

My baby spits up milk

Bringing up a little milk during or after a feed is very common.

How should my baby sleep safely?

A few simple habits lower the risk of sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS).

My baby wakes up a lot at night

Night waking is normal for babies.

Is my baby teething?

Most babies get their first tooth around 6 months, but anything from before 4 months to after 12 months can be normal.

When does my baby start solid food?

The World Health Organization (WHO) recommends starting solid food at around 6 months.

My baby is not crawling yet

Babies start to move around at very different ages, and some never crawl on hands and knees at all.

My baby has nappy rash

Nappy rash is common and can usually be treated at home.

Is my baby getting enough milk?

Many parents worry about this, especially in the first weeks of breastfeeding.

My newborn looks yellow

Yellowing of the skin or the whites of the eyes is called jaundice.

Is my baby's poo normal?

Baby poo changes a lot in the first week.

Babies and young children

My child has a high temperature

A temperature of 38°C or higher counts as a fever.

My baby has a cold

Young children catch many colds, especially in their first years of daycare.

My child is constipated

Constipation is common in babies and young children, especially when starting solid food or potty training.

How do I look after my baby's teeth?

Start brushing as soon as the first tooth appears.

My child bumped their head

Small bumps on the head are very common in toddlers and usually not serious.

What do I do if my child chokes?

It only takes a few minutes to learn what to do.

How do I make my home safe?

Babies move faster than you expect.

How do I protect my child in the sun?

Young skin burns easily.

Toddler and preschool

My toddler has tantrums

Tantrums are a normal part of growing up.

My toddler is a picky eater

It is perfectly normal for toddlers to refuse new foods or eat very little at some meals.

My child is not talking much yet

Children learn to talk at different ages.

When is my child ready for the potty?

Most children are ready to start between 18 months and 3 years, often around 2.

How much screen time is OK?

The World Health Organization (WHO) recommends sitting less and playing more for children under 5.

My toddler will not go to bed

Bedtime battles and night waking are common between 1 and 5 years.

Starting daycare

Starting daycare is a big step for children and parents.

My toddler bites or hits

Biting, hitting, pinching and hair-pulling are common in babies and toddlers.

For you

I have felt low since my baby was born

Feeling tearful, anxious or irritable in the first days after birth is very common.
