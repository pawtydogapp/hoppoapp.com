Why Hoppo is different

5 minutes, once a week

Not a daily to-do list. 1 small mission a week, on any day that suits you. It fits into bath time or breakfast.

Missed a week? Nothing is lost

No guilt and no red warnings. Hoppo picks up where you left off. Every child grows at their own pace, and so do parents.

Checked, not guessed

Every health statement is checked against at least 3 public sources, including the NHS, the World Health Organization and the American Academy of Pediatrics. Where they differ, Hoppo says so.

Your photos stay on your phone

Milestone photos are never uploaded. Your account data is stored in the European Union, and you can delete it all from the app at any time.

What is inside

100 skills, birth to 5

Tummy time, first words, pouring, buttons, taking turns. Each skill has simple steps, mistakes to avoid and other ways to practise.

188 milestones

Tick the first smile, first steps and first tooth. Add a photo and share a keepsake card with family.

Pregnancy week by week

Every week from 4 to 40 in plain words, with the size of your baby as an everyday fruit or vegetable, questions to ask your midwife, and a hospital bag checklist.
