Common questions

Is Hoppo really free?

Yes. Every skill, guide and feature is open to everyone. A small banner advert pays for the servers. Some links in Shop are affiliate links, which means Hoppo may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Is Hoppo medical advice?

No. Hoppo gives practice ideas, keeps your memories and explains what is common. It does not replace your doctor, midwife or health visitor. If you are worried about your child, contact them. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

My baby was born early. Which age does Hoppo use?

Open your child's profile in More and tick "Born early", then enter the original due date. Missions then follow the corrected age until age 2.

My baby has arrived. How do I switch from pregnancy?

On Home, tap "Baby arrived" and enter the date of birth. Hoppo turns the pregnancy into your baby's profile and moves to the first year.

Can I use Hoppo for more than one child?

Yes. Add each child, or a pregnancy, in More. Switch between them at the top of Home.

Can my partner use the same account?

Yes. Log in with the same email on both phones. Every entry is saved separately, so you can both log sleep or feeds at the same time without losing anything.

Where are my photos?

Photos stay on the phone where you added them and are never uploaded. If you change phone, you can pick them again from your gallery. Everything else, such as milestones, logs and the calendar, is saved to your account.

I do not get reminders.

Check that notifications for Hoppo are turned on in your phone settings. Then open More, then Settings, and choose the day and time for reminders.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to More, then Privacy and data, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your children's profiles, logs and calendar. If you cannot reach that screen, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

How do I report a mistake in a skill or guide?

Email us the name of the skill or guide and what looks wrong. Health and safety reports are checked first.
