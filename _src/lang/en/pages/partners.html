Partner with Hoppo

Hoppo reaches parents while they plan, prepare and settle in: from pregnancy to age 5. Our Shop puts your offer in front of them, inside the app and on this website.

The audience

Expecting parents and parents of babies and toddlers in the European Union, using the app in 11 languages. They open it weekly for a skill, a guide or a milestone.

The placement

A partner card in the Shop: your name, logo, offer, promo code and link. It shows natively, never as an interruption, and only to parents in countries you serve.

The tracking

Affiliate links through Awin, or a unique promo code per partner. Every sale is measurable on both sides.

Commercial models

We work with merchants on the Awin network, so tracking and payment follow your existing programme. Direct deals are possible too: a fixed fee per new customer, or a flat monthly placement. Rates are agreed per partner.

What works with parents

A percentage off the first order, free delivery, a free sample with a purchase, and a first month free for subscriptions. Useful categories are baby equipment and clothing, toys and books, food, services and insurance.

How we handle links

A tap on a partner card opens the phone browser, not a window inside Hoppo. The browser keeps your tracking cookie, so the sale is credited correctly. Offers and promo codes are served from our own database, so a change goes live the same day without an app release. Every affiliate link is disclosed to the parent next to the link.

What we do not list

We never list infant formula, because EU rules restrict its marketing. We also do not list products that make medical claims.

Hoppo family

Our own home made products will join the Shop when they are ready. They are sold on this website, never inside the app.
