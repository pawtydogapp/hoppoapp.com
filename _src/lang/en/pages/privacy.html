Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026  ·  Contact: info@hoppoapp.com

Hoppo is operated by Matej Mesko, Slovenia, who is the data controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This policy covers the Hoppo app for iPhone and Android and the website hoppoapp.com.

In short

  • We store what the app needs to work: your account, your children's profiles, pregnancy details, logs and calendar.
  • Some of this is health data. We only store it with your explicit consent, which you give when you create your account.
  • Photos stay on your phone. They are never uploaded.
  • Your data is stored in the European Union. We never sell it.
  • You can delete everything in the app at any time.

What we collect

Account. Your email address, a password, your country and language, and your app settings. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address from them. Passwords are handled and encrypted by our authentication provider, Supabase. We never see or store readable passwords.

Child and pregnancy profiles. A name or nickname, a date of birth or due date, and, if you choose, your child's sex (used only for the growth chart) and whether your baby was born early.

What you log. Weekly mission sessions and ratings, milestones you tick with their dates and notes, sleep and feeding entries, growth measurements (weight, length and head size), and calendar appointments with reminders.

Photos. Photos you add to milestones or profiles stay on your phone. They are never sent to us.

Purchases. We do not collect payment details. Hoppo has no paid features.

Health data and your explicit consent

Pregnancy details, growth measurements, and sleep and feeding logs can count as data concerning health. The GDPR gives this data extra protection. We process it only with your explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) of the GDPR, which you give when you create your account. We use it only to show it back to you in the app, for example in charts, weekly missions and reminders.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the entries, the profile, or your whole account. Withdrawing consent does not affect what happened before you withdrew it.

Why we use your data, and on what legal basis

  • Running your account and saving your entries: performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)), plus your explicit consent for health data (Article 9(2)(a)).
  • Usage statistics: our legitimate interest in knowing which parts of the app work (Article 6(1)(f)). See below.
  • Personalised advertising: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), given in the app. See below.
  • Answering your emails: our legitimate interest in helping you.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your email address, your children's profiles, your logs or your calendar with advertisers or shops. We do not use your children's data for advertising.

Advertising

The app shows a small banner advert through Google AdMob. This keeps Hoppo free.

Pregnancy and health screens. Adverts on pregnancy screens and health screens, such as the guides and the sleep, feeding and growth logs, are always non-personalised. They are not chosen based on you.

Other screens. When you first open the app, you are asked whether Hoppo may use your data for personalised advertising. On iPhone you also see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Adverts are only personalised if you agree. If you consent, your device advertising identifier is shared with Google and its advertising partners, with approximate location and information about how you use the app.

If you decline, you see the same number of adverts, chosen without using your data. No feature is held back.

Changing your mind. In the app, open More, then Privacy and data, then Ad privacy options. On iPhone you can also go to Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Tracking. On Android, go to Settings, then Google, then Ads.

Google acts as an independent controller for the advertising data it receives, under Google's own privacy policy. The website shows no adverts.

Shop and affiliate links

The Shop in the app lists offers from partner shops. Some links are affiliate links, managed through the Awin network. If you tap one, the shop and Awin may set a cookie in your browser to record a later purchase. We do not receive your name, address or payment details from these shops, only reports of how many sales were made. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave Hoppo.

Orders from the Hoppo shop

If you buy a Hoppo family product on hoppoapp.com, we collect your name, delivery address, email address, an optional phone number and what you ordered. We use them to take payment, deliver your order, send receipts, handle returns and keep the accounting records the law requires. The legal basis is the contract with you and our legal obligations (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(c)).

Payment is handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach Hoppo. Stripe has its own privacy policy. Delivery companies receive your name, address and phone number so they can deliver.

You buy without a Hoppo account. We do not link your order to your account or to your children's profiles, and we do not use your order details for advertising. We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires and delete them afterwards.

Usage statistics

We collect usage statistics through PostHog, hosted in the European Union. For example, we count which screens are opened and how many missions are saved. These events are linked to a random account number, never to your name or email address. They can include which skill or milestone an event is about, and whether a profile is a pregnancy or a child. They do not include your logs, measurements, notes or photos. The app does not use cookies for this.

The website does not use cookies and does not store anything on your device.

Notifications

Reminders for appointments and weekly missions are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone settings.

Where your data is stored

Your account and entries are stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany, in the European Union. Usage statistics are stored with PostHog in the European Union. Where a provider may access data from outside the European Union, they use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address when you visit, to deliver the page and keep the service secure. Fonts are served from hoppoapp.com itself.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed straight away, and from backups within 30 days. Usage statistics are kept for up to 12 months. Emails to us are kept for as long as needed to answer them, and no longer than 2 years.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and move your data, and to object to how we use it. You can also withdraw any consent at any time.

  • Correct your data: edit profiles and entries directly in the app.
  • Delete everything: open More, then Privacy and data, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your children's profiles, logs and calendar.
  • Get a copy of your data: email us and we will send it within 30 days.

You also have the right to complain to the Slovenian Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec), or to the data protection authority in your own country.

Children

Hoppo is for parents and carers aged 18 and over. Children do not use the app themselves. The data about a child is entered by their parent or carer, who decides what to add and can delete it at any time.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and show the new effective date at the top. If a change affects how we use health data, we will ask for your consent again in the app.
