2. What these terms cover

These terms cover orders for Hoppo family products on hoppoapp.com. Partner shops listed in the Marketplace sell under their own terms. A purchase from a partner shop is a contract between you and that shop.

3. Prices

Prices are in euros and are final. The seller is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is charged. Delivery costs and any discount are shown in your cart before you pay.

4. How to order

Add products to your cart, choose your country, enter a promo code if you have one, and pay on the secure Stripe page. You enter your name and delivery address there. Your order is confirmed when your payment succeeds and Stripe sends your receipt by email. We may cancel and refund an order if a product is unavailable or a price was shown by mistake.

5. Payment

Payment is handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach Hoppo.

6. Delivery

We deliver within the European Union. The delivery cost for your country and the expected delivery time are shown in your cart. The goods become your risk when you receive them.

7. Your right to withdraw

You may withdraw from your order within 14 days after you receive the goods, without giving a reason. Tell us clearly by email, for example "I withdraw from my order number 1001". Send the goods back within 14 days after you tell us. We refund the price and the standard delivery cost within 14 days after we receive the goods, or after you show that you sent them. You pay the cost of returning the goods unless we agree otherwise.

The right does not apply to goods made to your specification, or to sealed goods that cannot be returned for hygiene reasons once you have opened them.

Please return the goods in the condition you received them. You are responsible for any loss in value caused by handling beyond what is needed to check the goods.

8. Faulty goods

EU law gives you a legal guarantee of at least 2 years from delivery. If a product is faulty or not as described, contact us. You are entitled to a repair, a replacement, a price reduction or a refund, under the rules of your country.

9. Promo codes

A promo code has no cash value and applies to products, not to goods already ordered. You can use 1 code per order. A code may have an end date, a minimum order or a limited number of uses. We may withdraw a code at any time. A code already applied to a confirmed order stays valid for that order.

10. Safety, care and handmade products

Read the age and care information that comes with each product. Always supervise babies and children with toys, textiles and small parts.

Some Hoppo family products are made by hand. Colour, pattern and size can differ slightly from the photos and from pair to pair. This is not a defect. The product page states the composition, the measurements and the care instructions, and the same information is on the label. Measure your baby's foot before you order.

Check handmade textiles for loose threads before each use. Stop using the product if a thread comes loose or the stitching opens.

11. Your data

How we use your order details is explained in our Privacy Policy.

12. Law and complaints

Slovenian law applies, without taking away the consumer protection of the country where you live. If you have a complaint, write to us first. We will answer within 14 days.
